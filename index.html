01 / THE CLUB

FROM DEDZA TO AFRICA.

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos FC represents the passion, ambition and football culture of Dedza, Malawi.

Known as The Yellow Boys , the club competes in the FDH Bank Premiership.

The club represents Dedza on the national football stage while building a strong identity around football, community and competitive ambition.

The club's home ground is Dedza Stadium .
