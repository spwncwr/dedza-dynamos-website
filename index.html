01 / THE CLUB

FROM DEDZA TO AFRICA.

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos FC represents the passion, ambition and football culture of Dedza, Malawi.

Known as The Yellow Boys , the club continues to build a football identity rooted in community, discipline and competitive ambition.

The club's journey represents more than football. It is about developing talent, inspiring young people and proudly carrying Dedza's name onto the national stage.

05 / CLUB HISTORY

HISTORY MADE.

2026

FIRST CASTEL CHALLENGE CUP FINAL

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos wrote one of the biggest chapters in the club's history during the 2025/26 Castel Challenge Cup.

After defeating Creck Sporting 3–1, overcoming Ekhaya FC 2–0 and defeating Blue Eagles 4–3 on penalties, the Yellow Boys reached their first-ever Castel Challenge Cup final.

In the final at Bingu National Stadium, Dedza fought hard against FCB Nyasa Big Bullets before narrowly losing 2–1.
