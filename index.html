01 / THE CLUB

FROM DEDZA TO AFRICA.

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos FC represents the passion, ambition and football culture of Dedza, Malawi.

Known as The Yellow Boys, the club is building a football identity rooted in discipline, community and competitive ambition.

Our mission is to develop football talent, inspire young people and proudly represent Dedza on the national and international stage.
