01 / THE CLUB

FROM DEDZA TO AFRICA.

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos FC represents the passion, ambition and football culture of Dedza, Malawi.

Known as The Yellow Boys, the club is building an identity rooted in discipline, community and competitive ambition.

Our mission is to develop football talent, inspire young people and represent Dedza proudly on the national and international stage.
