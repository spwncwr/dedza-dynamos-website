01 / THE CLUB

FROM DEDZA TO AFRICA.

Goshen City Dedza Dynamos FC represents the passion, ambition and football culture of Dedza, Malawi.

Known as The Yellow Boys , the club continues to build a football identity rooted in community, discipline and competitive ambition.

Our vision is to develop football talent, inspire young people and proudly represent Dedza on the national and international stage.
